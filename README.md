# NSDS-Zen-x-mini-setup
- Connect CSI cable(white cable) at MIPI port metal side facing down
- Connect CSI cable to carrier board, metal side facing the pins
- Connect GMSL2(blue cable) cable's female side to the camera, wait for click
- With capture card powered off, connect other end of GMSL2 cable to capture card's male end. Wait for click
- Connect power adapter cable to white PWR J3 connecter 
- Connect HDMI/Keyboard/Mouse to USB
- Power up and wait for boot
