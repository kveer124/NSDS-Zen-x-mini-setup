# NSDS-Zen-x-mini-setup
1. Connect power adapter cable to white PWR J3 connector
2. Connect CSI cable at MIPI port metal side facing down
3. Connect CSI cable to carrier board, metal side facing the pins
4. Connect GMSL2 cable's female side to the camera, wait for click
5. With capture card powered off, connect other end of GMSL2 cable to capture card's male end. Wait for click
6. Plug the power for the capture card afer cables are connected
7. Connect HDMI/Keyboard/Mouse to USB
8. Power up and wait for boot
